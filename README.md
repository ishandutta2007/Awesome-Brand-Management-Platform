# Awesome-Brand-Management-Platform

# Top Brand Management Platform Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Brand Asset Management, Digital Asset Management & Creative Workflows*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Brand Management**. These tools help organizations store, organize, search, share, and distribute brand assets like logos, images, videos, documents, and brand guidelines to ensure consistency across teams and channels.

**Examples** include Frontify, Brandfolder, Bynder, Marq, Ziflow, Filestage, Acquia DAM, Canto, MediaValet, BrandMaker, BrandMaster, IntelligenceBank, and Brandworkz (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosting, custom asset workflows, and transparent brand data management — ideal for marketing teams, creative agencies, and developers building vendor-independent brand management solutions. Note that while several mature open-source DAM platforms exist, the ecosystem is more fragmented than in some other categories, with many solutions being general-purpose file managers or photo organizers that require customization for enterprise brand asset management.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Frontify](https://www.frontify.com/)**  
  Brand management platform combining DAM with brand guidelines, design templates, and collaboration tools for distributed teams.

- **[Brandfolder](https://brandfolder.com/)**  
  Digital asset management platform with AI-powered search, brand portals, and integrations for marketing workflows.

- **[Bynder](https://www.bynder.com/)**  
  Enterprise DAM with brand templating, creative workflow automation, and analytics. Offers a local Docker version for self-hosting with license .

- **[Marq](https://www.marq.com/)**  
  Brand templating and design platform enabling teams to create on-brand marketing materials without design expertise.

- **[Ziflow](https://www.ziflow.com/)**  
  Creative proofing and approval platform for managing feedback and version control on brand assets.

- **[Filestage](https://filestage.io/)**  
  Content review and approval platform for marketing and creative teams to streamline brand asset feedback.

- **[Acquia DAM](https://www.acquia.com/products/dam)**  
  Enterprise DAM integrated with Drupal, offering brand portals, asset delivery, and marketing workflow tools.

- **[Canto](https://www.canto.com/)**  
  DAM platform focused on ease of use, with facial recognition, custom portals, and metadata management for brand assets.

- **[MediaValet](https://www.mediavalet.com/)**  
  Cloud-native DAM built for enterprise with AI, workflow automation, and brand compliance tools.

- **[BrandMaker](https://www.brandmaker.com/)**  
  Marketing resource management platform with brand asset management and campaign planning capabilities.

- **[BrandMaster](https://www.brandmaster.com/)**  
  Online platform to plan, collect, share, adapt, and distribute all marketing materials, with comprehensive brand management features .

- **[IntelligenceBank](https://www.intelligencebank.com/)**  
  DAM with marketing compliance, brand portals, and creative workflow management.

- **[Brandworkz](https://www.brandworkz.com/)**  
  Brand asset management and marketing operations platform for enterprise brand consistency.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[ResourceSpace](https://github.com/resourcespace/resourcespace)**  
  The most widely adopted open-source DAM, with a strong focus on asset organization and sharing. Organize, manage, and share brand assets like logos, images, videos, and guidelines. Mature platform with 250,000+ users including Coca-Cola, Google, and Toyota. Web and mobile access, multilingual, 24/7 availability, unlimited users. Free open-source license with no vendor lock-in. Ideal for public organizations, research centers, museums, or SMEs seeking a functional DAM at low cost .

- **[Pimcore (Community Edition)](https://github.com/pimcore/pimcore)**  
  Open-source platform for Product Information Management (PIM), Master Data Management (MDM), and Digital Asset Management (DAM). Can manage brand assets alongside product data. Used by over 82,000 companies in 56 countries including Pepsi, Lego, and Audi. Gartner "Cool Vendor" recipient. Free without license costs, supports unlimited file formats and data volumes. Hybrid DAM-PIM-CMS approach reduces integration overhead for multichannel catalogs and e-commerce .

- **[Phraseanet](https://github.com/alchemy-fr/Phraseanet)**  
  Open-source DAM solution for photos, videos, audio, and documents. Features Elasticsearch engine, AI-assisted batch indexing, thesaurus, metadata read/write (IPTC, XMP, DC, ID, MP3), video chaptering and subtitling, rights management, workflows, web galleries, sharing, publishing, and plugins for WordPress and Drupal. Data hosted in France with GDPR compliance .

- **[Damvia](https://github.com/damviaHQ/damvia)**  
  Open-source Digital Asset Management (DAM) solution that works natively with your existing Cloud Storage. Seamlessly works with Dropbox, OneDrive Business, and more. Features PIM to DAM automatic linking, interactive folder pages, and advanced search using product metadata. Released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 .

- **[Longpoint](https://github.com/longpoint-io/longpoint)**  
  Extensible, self-hosted, API-first digital asset management system with intelligence features. Robust media pipeline with automatic optimization, secure delivery via URL signing and CDN integration. Features semantic search, automatic classification (scene detection, tagging), and multimodal understanding for images, videos, audio, and documents. Bring your own models (Claude, GPT, open source). FSL/MIT licensed, extensible through plugins .

- **[UnoPim DAM](https://github.com/unopim/unopim-digital-asset-management)**  
  Flexible, open-source Digital Asset Management system built on Laravel, designed for the UnoPim PIM ecosystem. Features file and directory management with visual gallery grid, asset upload/preview/deletion, advanced metadata tagging, collaboration tools with comments, and CSV/XLSX export for asset assignment. Supports video and PDF thumbnail generation via ffmpeg and poppler-utils. Secure asset handling rejects executable and HTML/script file types .

- **[Brand Assets Ecosystem](https://github.com/b-ciq/brand-assets-ecosystem)**  
  Multi-interface brand asset management system with enhanced search, metadata, and deployment capabilities. Includes web GUI (Next.js), MCP server for AI agent integration, and shared SDK. Features asset browsing, search, download, and human-readable asset names. Planned Slack integration. Architecture separates core MCP development from web GUI and shared TypeScript interfaces .

- **[Brand DNA](https://www.npmjs.com/package/brand-dna)**  
  Opinionated, open-source source of truth for a brand — structured for AI, presented as a useful brandbook for people. Maintains one public set of brand decisions and assets behind two views: brand-dna.json + assets for machines, and a public brandbook for people. Covers purpose, positioning, audiences, voice, color palette, typography, logo variants, imagery directions, and accessibility. Designed to live inside an existing repository and integrate with AI workflows .

- **[brandspec](https://www.npmjs.com/package/brandspec)**  
  Define Brand Identity as code. Open format built on W3C Design Tokens. Features AI-guided brand workshop, brand.yaml for structured decisions, and connections to CSS, Tailwind, Figma, Style Dictionary, and AI consultation. MIT licensed. Includes CLI for validation, generation, and consultation .

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **mcp-tool-shop-org/brand** — Centralized brand asset registry for GitHub organizations. One repo holds every logo; every README points to it via raw.githubusercontent.com URLs. Solves duplication, drift, and inconsistency. CLI for verify, manifest, stats, audit, and migrate. Daily GitHub Action auto-syncs new logos .
- **Nuxeo DAM** — Built on open source foundations, accommodates billions of documents with flexible customization. AI-enhanced modeling with automatic tagging, metadata, and relationships. Supports graphic, video, 3D, and VR assets .
- **Dash** — WordPress-integrated DAM for growing brands. Search and insert visual content from Dash without leaving WordPress. Free trial available .

**Frameworks for building custom brand management solutions**: For enterprise-grade open-source DAM, **ResourceSpace** provides the most mature foundation with proven scalability and brand asset management capabilities . For organizations needing integrated PIM/MDM/DAM, **Pimcore Community Edition** offers broader data management beyond assets alone . For cloud-native self-hosted DAM with existing storage, **Damvia** works natively with Dropbox and OneDrive . For AI-native extensible media management, **Longpoint** provides semantic search and automatic classification . For development teams wanting code-first brand infrastructure, **Brand DNA** and **brandspec** integrate brand decisions directly into repositories and AI workflows . For GitHub-native brand asset consistency, **mcp-tool-shop-org/brand** solves org-wide logo duplication . Note that true enterprise brand management with approval workflows, creative automation, and marketing integrations remains largely commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong asset organization and sharing foundations that require customization for full brand operations.
